Chiedza Bombe CA(SA) is a 34-year-old who is the regional general manager at VanHuys Pty (Ltd) ('VanHuys'). She has been working there for 10 years. Chiedza is married to Ifeoma Bombe out of community of property. They have three adopted children, Maziko (16), Mzati (12) and Luke (6). Chiedza, Ifeoma and their children are all residents for South African tax purposes.

VanHuys is a hotel company with multiple hotels in and around the Western Cape. The company's mission is to provide accommodation for any traveller. The company's hotel room offerings range from budget-friendly rooms to high-end suites, so anyone, regardless of budget, can enjoy the Western Cape to the fullest. The company is a category B VAT vendor with a financial year end of 31 December.

VanHuys hired a new financial accountant who has recently graduated from the University of Cape Town. She has put together the following draft calculation of Chiedza's remuneration:

After seeing some videos of professional skateboarder Tony Hawk, Luke decided he wanted to try doing an alpha flip (a type of skateboarding trick) himself. He attempted the trick but unfortunately broke his arm. Chiedza and Ifeoma quickly rushed him to the hospital. The medical costs amounted to R25 000. Chiedza's medical aid covered 60% of the cost, and VanHuys paid for the balance.

2. Chiedza manages multiple hotels in the area and must travel between hotels often for work. VanHuys bought a VW Tiguan (with a maintenance plan) on 1 March 2023 for R480 000 and immediately granted Chiedza the right to use this car. She has had the right to use this car for the entire 2025 year of assessment.
Chiedza fell ill and was put on bed rest from 1 June to 15 June. She did not use the car during those days. Chiedza drove for a total of 46 000 kilometres for the year, 75% of this related to business travel. She incurred the following costs throughout the year: insurance of R20 000, licensing fees of R500 and fuel of R50 000.

3. On 1 August 2024, Chiedza attended a hotel managers' convention in Durban. She spent 5 days and 4 nights there. VanHuys paid for her return flight, which cost R7 000. They also paid Chiedza an allowance of R15 000 to cover her accommodation, meals and incidental costs. VanHuys required that she provide proof of her expenditure after the trip, but she did not need to refund any unused amounts. Chiedza stayed with her aunt while in Durban and spent R500 per day on meals and incidentals. An average hotel room in the area would have cost R1 000 a night.

4. VanHuys bought a MacBook Air that cost R20 000 on 1 March 2024 and gave Chiedza the right to use it. Chiedza will have the right to use the laptop for 5 years, which is its estimated useful life. Chiedza will use the laptop for both work and private purposes, but mainly for private purposes.

5. Chiedza has an elderly grandmother who was granted the right to live in a deluxe suite owned by VanHuys for free on 1 March 2024 for the full year of assessment. The deluxe suite has an open-plan kitchen, one bedroom, a luxurious bathroom, and unobstructed views of the city. The room is fully furnished, and electricity is provided.

6. VanHuys entered into an agreement with KDB Bank that allowed its employees to take out loans at an interest rate of 5%. In addition to negotiating this special rate, VanHuys will also be paying 2% of the interest on any of the loans extended to its employees. On 25 November 2024, Chiedza received an email from an Irish lawyer informing her that she had a distant great aunt who had recently passed away. This aunt had left her castle in the countryside of Carricklea, Ireland, to Chiedza. However, before the property could be transferred into her name, Chiedza would have to pay the lawyer R200 000. Overjoyed, Chiedza immediately applied for a loan from KDB bank. A loan of R200 000 was granted to her at the special interest rate on 1 December 2024. The market value of this property was R3 500 000 (correctly translated) on 1 January 2025 when it was registered in Chiedza's name. Chiedza's great aunt was not, and had never been, a resident of South Africa for tax purposes. The total loan was still outstanding at the end of the year of assessment.

PageswithChi
During the lockdown in 2020, Chiedza and Ifeoma reignited their love for reading. Chiedza read over 50 books in 3 months. She decided to start sharing her monthly reads on her TikTok account, PageswithChi, and quickly amassed a large following of other avid readers. In her free time, she is a "BookTok" influencer (a TikTok community where readers share and discuss books). She films videos sharing what she is currently reading and looking forward to reading, and will occasionally post content sponsored by brands.

To further improve the quality of her videos, on 1 September 2024, Chiedza decided to purchase a Sony Alpha A7 camera for R25 000.

Chiedza received gifts from various brands totalling a market value of R40 000. These gifts were given to her without there being any obligation to create content. After seeing how engaging her audience is, a local book shop, Books4Bargain, approached Chiedza to film five promotional videos for them. They gave her a script and a minimum length for the videos. Chiedza was paid R1 000 per video and also received a gift voucher of R6 000 that she could spend at Books4Bargain.

One of Chiedza's videos went viral and drove considerable traffic to Books4Bargain's website. Books4Bargain was so impressed with her work that they are considering contracting her on a more regular basis. They would like her to produce 8 TikTok videos each month for their company account. Chiedza will be given a brief on what to include in each video. She will also have to film the videos in Books4Bargain stores. Chiedza would have to submit each video to the Books4Bargain social media manager for approval.

To celebrate their 15th wedding anniversary, Chiedza and Ifeoma decided to take a one-week trip to the Maldives. They are both adventurous and took full advantage of all the activities available on the island. While jet skiing, Chiedza got into an accident and broke her spine. Unfortunately, after a six-hour surgery, Chiedza succumbed to her injury and passed away on 28 February 2025.

less than actual value  |  1

VAT
An enterprise as defined in the VAT act excludes employees who earn remunerations
This implies that Steward cannot be a VAT vendor and is not allowed to charge output VAT on services
rendered to the company
It follows that there are no VAT consequences for Steward on the hamper awarded to him  |  1

VAT

When Stacked awards fringe benefits, generally it would be a deemed supply on which output VAT is
payable in terms of s18(3)  |  1
The awarding of the hamper is however not a deemed supply as it is a supply of entertainment.  |  1
It follows that no output VAT is payable  |  0,5

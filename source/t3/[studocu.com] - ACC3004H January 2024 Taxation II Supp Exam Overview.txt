QUESTION 1  |  (43 MARKS; 65 MINUTES)
Dune Adventures (Pty) Ltd is a company that manufactures and sells sandboards and other
sandboarding equipment and offers sandboarding1 in the Western Cape. The company was
established by siblings, Rogan and Fatiema Malambe, in 2018 as they wanted to turn their passion
into a business. Rogan is a 50% shareholder and is still involved in the day-to-day operations of the
company whereas Fatiema is no longer involved in the operations but still owns 50% of the shares.
The company has a 31 October year-end and has been registered as a Category B VAT vendor since
it was incorporated in 2018.
The company is based at the Atlantis Dunes in Atlantis, Western Cape. On the premises, the company
has its retail shop and administration office. The manufacturing facility is also located in Atlantis.
Due to the location of the sandboarding and many people not wanting to drive so far by themselves,
the company also offers transport from Cape Town CBD to the dunes. This is an optional extra that
customers pay for separately.
The company requires your assistance with the finalisation of its tax returns. The following information
is relevant:
1. The company ordered a new sand board manufacturing machine from a company in Germany
in August 2023 and it arrived in South Africa on 2 October 2023. The machine was only cleared
for home consumption on 14 October 2023. The cost of the machine was R19 564 (correctly
converted to Rands). Import duties of R3 913 was paid and the customs duty value was
R22 000. The company paid a local delivery company R2 500 to deliver the machine to the
manufacturing premises; it arrived on 20 October 2023, and it was brought into use on that
same date.
2. For the sandboarding activity, each participant is charged R995 for the half day activity. This
price includes the sandboarding activity and a drink on arrival. There is no restaurant at the
dunes, but there is a cash bar where customers can buy drinks and snacks. During the current
year, 10 950 participants took part in the sandboarding activity. The total revenue received
during this period from the cash bar was R4 700 000, the total costs of the drinks and snacks
was R1 175 000, including the drink customers received on arrival.
3. The company earned R5 475 000 during the period transporting customers from the CBD to
the dunes and back to the CBD.
4. The company employs 25 people in total who work in the different departments. Some assist
with the driving to and from the dunes, the driving to the top of the dunes and helping
customers with the gear and the activity on the day. Some staff work in the retail shop or the
manufacturing department. One employee does the booking of the sandboarding activity and
transport. The total salaries for the year of assessment amounted to R3 150 000.
5. The company rents an office for R5 000 per month, where all the company’s administration is
performed.
6. The company has tax-deductible expenditure (correctly calculated) of R9 887 500.
7. During the current year of assessment, the retail store was almost robbed. One of the
employees, who only works in the retail store, managed to catch the robbers and locked them

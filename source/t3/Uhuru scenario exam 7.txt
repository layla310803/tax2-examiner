Uhuru  |  (37 MARKS, 56 MINUTES)
Uhuru (Pty) Ltd (‘Uhuru’) is a company resident in South Africa for income tax purposes and
a registered value-added tax (VAT) vendor. Uhuru is a South African law firm based in
Johannesburg and Durban. The company’s financial year ends on 31 December.
All amounts are exclusive of VAT unless otherwise stated or shown by the context.
Nomveliso Zungu
Nomveliso Zungu (‘Nomveliso’) is a South African lawyer at Uhuru, aged 55 years old.
Nomveliso joined Uhuru on 1 March 2023 and has since 1 November 2024 been based at the
Durban office of Uhuru.
Nomveliso holds a Masters in Law in Constitutional Law (LLM) from the University of Cape
Town and has recently been appointed by Uhuru as its Business Leader on matters regarding
section 25 of the South African Constitution which deals with property rights.
Nomveliso’s remuneration package with Uhuru entitles her to the following benefits, which
have remained consistent from the 2024 year of assessment:
• She receives a monthly salary of R230 000.
• Uhuru contributes R5 000 per month towards the company’s pension fund on Nomveliso’s
behalf. Nomveliso makes no other contributions.
• Uhuru makes medical aid contributions in respect of Nomveliso and her minor son, who
is a dependant on her medical aid. During the year of assessment Uhuru made
contributions of R69 000 on behalf of Nomveliso and her son. Nomveliso incurred
R90 000 of qualifying medical expenses that was not recovered from the medical aid
scheme.
• Uhuru pays for Nomveliso’s son’s school fees. Nomveliso’s son attends Hilton College, a
high school in the Midlands in KwaZulu Natal. The school is registered in terms of the
South African Schools Act 1996. The school fees are R20 000 per month.
• Nomveliso receives a travel allowance of R6 000 per month. During the year of
assessment, she travelled a total of 20 000km, 16 000km of which related to business
travel. She kept an accurate logbook for her travels in her BMW 1 series which cost her
R327 000 (including VAT). The BMW 1 series was purchased in 2023 and the purchase
price included a 5-year maintenance plan. Nomveliso provided her logbook to her
employer on a monthly basis. The employer was satisfied based on the previous year that
the kms travelled each month were reflective of business travel and the km were travelled
evenly over the period. During the year of assessment Nomveliso incurred fuel costs of
R10 000 relating to the BMW 1 series.
• Uhuru allows all employees to use the company gym in its building all year round. The
market value of this benefit at an equivalent gym would have cost Nomveliso R699 per
month. The construction costs of the gym incurred by Uhuru was R890 000.
In addition to the above, during the 2025 year of assessment Nomveliso received the following
extra-ordinary benefits from Uhuru:

On 1 November 2024 Nomveliso was transferred from the Johannesburg offices of Uhuru to
the Durban offices. As a result, Uhuru incurred relocation costs of R90 000 (including VAT),
paid to Stuttafords Van Liners (Pty) Ltd, for the transportation of Nomveliso’s household goods
from Johannesburg to Durban. Nomveliso’s goods were transported by road.
Stuttafords Van Liners (Pty) Ltd invoiced Nomveliso for these costs but the amount was settled
by Uhuru. Stuttafords Van Liners (Pty) Ltd is a resident in South Africa for income tax
purposes and a registered value-added tax (VAT) vendor.
Nomveliso has also received the following additional income:
• She holds a purchased annuity from the South African investment firm, Corporation Ltd,
which she acquired for R200 000 in 2021. The annuity pays R3 000 per month for the next
25 years.
• From 1 February 2024 she received an annuity of R10 000 per month from her ex-husband
for the maintenance of their son.
Restructuring of Nomveliso’s Remuneration Package
Uhuru noticed that Nomveliso’s car was experiencing many functional issues. In February
2025 it offered her the choice of either using the company’s Toyota Corolla or of increasing
her travel allowance received to R9 000 per month from 1 March 2025. The Toyota Corolla
was acquired by Uhuru on 1 March 2023 with no maintenance plan for R322 900 (including
VAT). Nomveliso would be granted the use of the car from 1 March 2025 until 28 February
2026. She is not sure which option is the most tax beneficial for her.
It would be expected that with the new Toyota Corolla she would travel 25 000km from 1 March
2025 to 28 February 2026, 18 000km of which will relate to business travel. With the travel
allowance she would continue to use her BMW 1 series and would travel the same distances
as in the company car. It is expected that she will spend R12 000 on fuel for either vehicle,
and that she will keep a record of her fuel expenditure.
Takunda Bhezuzi
On 1 September 2024 Uhuru employed Takunda Bhezuzi (‘Takunda’), a lawyer from
Zimbabwe. Takunda is a legal expert on farming rights in Zimbabwe and was employed by
Uhuru to assist with various South African farming matters for the next nine months. Whilst
Takunda was in South Africa, Uhuru paid the rental costs per month of a Westville property it
leased, the rental value correctly calculated is R20 000. Takunda is not a South African
resident and has never previously travelled to South Africa.

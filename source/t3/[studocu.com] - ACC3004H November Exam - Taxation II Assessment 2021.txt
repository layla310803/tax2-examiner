QUESTION 1 (65 MARKS; 98 MINUTES)
CT Glass and Aluminium (Pty) Ltd (‘CT Glass’ or ‘the company’) is a South African manufacturer of
aluminium and glass products. The company manufactures, supplies, and installs a wide range of
products that include doors, windows, and shopfronts, amongst others. The company offers both
standard products as well as custom designs to suit customers’ needs. Since 2018, the company has
been exporting standard windows and doors to other African countries.
CT Glass was founded by the Abrahams siblings in 2011 and has since operated as a family-run
business. Each sibling has a 25% shareholding in the company, however, only the two brothers, Abdul
and Yazied are involved in the operations of the company. Both brothers are Chartered Accountants
(South Africa). CT Glass is a registered Category C VAT Vendor and has a 31 March financial year-
end. The company is a small business corporation as defined in terms of s12E(4) of the Income Tax
Act.
The company has requested your assistance with regards to various matters concerning its tax affairs
as the financial manager is on maternity leave.
All amounts are inclusive of VAT where appropriate unless stated otherwise or shown by the
context.
Total sales for the financial year-end amounted to R15 650 000 (including VAT); of these total sales
35% was cash sales and the remainder related to credit sales. 25% of the total sales relates to
windows and doors that were exported to other African countries.

The employee requested CT Glass to pay for his medical costs however the company refused
to pay the employee any compensation for his medical costs and argued that he was operating
the grinder at a speed above the maximum. The employee sued the company, and the
company hired a lawyer to defend them. Upon investigation, it was found that the employee

compensation to the employee and the legal fees to its lawyer during February 2021.

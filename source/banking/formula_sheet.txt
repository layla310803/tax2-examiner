Z-SCORE
$$Z = 1.2X_1 + 1.4X_2 + 3.3X_3 + 0.6X_4 + 1.0X_5$$
$X_1$ = Net working capital / total assets
$X_2$ = Retained earnings / total assets
$X_3$ = Earnings before interest and taxes / total assets
$X_4$ = Market value of equity / book value of total liabilities
$X_5$ = Sales / total assets

LOGIT PROBABILITY OF DEFAULT

DURATION AND CAPITAL AT RISK

$$\Delta L = -D^* \times L \times \Delta R$$
$$(\varphi_T - \varphi_F) \times D^* \times A$$

RAROC (RISK SHEET)

CAPITAL AND LIQUIDITY
$$\text{CET1 ratio} = \frac{\text{Common Equity Tier 1 capital}}{\text{Risk-weighted assets}}$$
$$\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{High-quality liquid assets (HQLA)}}{\text{Total net cash outflows over the next 30 days}}$$

RAROC
$$\text{RAROC} = \left(\text{ROA} - \frac{\text{Expected loss}}{\text{Earning assets}}\right) \times \frac{\text{Earning assets}}{\text{Total assets}} \times \frac{\text{Total assets}}{\text{Risk-required capital}}$$
$$\text{RAROC} = \frac{\text{One-year net income on a loan}}{\text{Capital at risk}}$$

DURATION (page 1)
$$D_{\text{perpetuity}} = \frac{1 + y}{y}$$
$$D_{\text{annuity}} = \frac{1 + y}{y} - \frac{T}{(1 + y)^T - 1}$$
$$D_{\text{coupon bond}} = \frac{1 + y}{y} - \frac{(1 + y) + T(c - y)}{c\left[(1 + y)^T - 1\right] + y}$$
$$D_{\text{par bond}} = \frac{1 + y}{y}\left[1 - \frac{1}{(1 + y)^T}\right]$$
where $y$ = yield, $c$ = coupon rate, $T$ = years to maturity

DURATION (page 2)
$$D = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{N} CF_t \times DF_t \times t}{\sum_{t=1}^{N} CF_t \times DF_t} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{N} PV_t \times t}{\sum_{t=1}^{N} PV_t}$$
$$D_{\text{semi-annual}} = \frac{\sum_{t=1/2}^{N} CF_t \times t \,/\, (1 + R/2)^{2t}}{\sum_{t=1/2}^{N} CF_t \,/\, (1 + R/2)^{2t}}$$

MARKET RISK AND PORTFOLIO THEORY
$$\bar{R}_i = \sum_{i=1}^{N} P_i \times HR_i \quad \text{(expected return)}$$
$$\sigma_i = \sqrt{\sigma_i^2}$$
$$\bar{R}_p = \sum_{i=1}^{N} X_i \times HR_i \quad \text{(portfolio return)}$$
$$\sigma_p = \sqrt{X_i^2\sigma_i^2 + X_j^2\sigma_j^2 + 2X_iX_j\sigma_i\sigma_j\rho_{ij}}$$
(The printed sheet has typos here, a stray "b²" and "K" for X; the formula above is the standard two-asset one.)
$$\rho_{ij} = \frac{\sigma_{ij}}{\sigma_i \sigma_j}$$
$$CV = \frac{\sigma}{\mu} \quad \text{(coefficient of variation)}$$

LOAN PRICING
$$1 + k = 1 + \frac{of + (BR + \varphi)}{1 - [b(1 - RR)]}$$
where $of$ = origination fee, $BR$ = base lending rate, $\varphi$ = risk premium, $b$ = compensating balance requirement, $RR$ = reserve requirement

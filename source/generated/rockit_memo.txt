Required 1: Vendor Registration Requirements
Marking Point / Principle  |  Marks
In terms of section 23(1)(a) of the VAT Act, a person carrying on an enterprise is compulsorily liable to register as a VAT vendor at the end of any month where the total value of taxable supplies made in the 12 months ending at the end of that month has exceeded R1 million.  |  1
Alternatively, in terms of section 23(1)(b), compulsory registration is required at the commencement of any month where there are reasonable grounds for believing that taxable supplies in the next 12 months will exceed R1 million in terms of a written contractual obligation.  |  1
In terms of section 23(3)(b), a person may voluntarily register if taxable supplies made have exceeded R50 000 in the preceding 12-month period (or are reasonably expected to exceed R50 000 within 12 months).  |  0,5
A person liable to register compulsorily must apply within 21 days of becoming liable (section 23(2)).  |  0,5

Required 2: Direct vs. Indirect Taxes
Marking Point / Principle  |  Marks
Direct taxes are levied directly on the income, profits or wealth of a person who bears the ultimate legal and economic burden.  |  0,5
Example of a direct tax: normal income tax, capital gains tax, donations tax or estate duty.  |  0,5
Indirect taxes are levied on transactions, goods or services; the vendor collects and pays them over, but the economic burden is passed on to the final consumer.  |  0,5
Example of an indirect tax: VAT, customs duty, excise duty or transfer duty.  |  0,5

Required 3: VAT Consequences of Vehicle Purchases (Note 4)
Marking Point / Principle  |  Marks
Double-cab bakkie (R640 000):
In terms of section 1 of the VAT Act, a "motor car" includes a double-cab light delivery vehicle.  |  1
The double-cab bakkie therefore falls within the definition of a motor car.  |  0,5
In terms of section 17(2)(c), input tax is denied on any motor car supplied to a vendor, unless acquired by a car dealer for resale, a car rental enterprise, etc.  |  1
Even though the bakkie is used for deliveries of taxable sales, none of the exceptions apply; input tax is denied in full (input tax = R0).  |  0,5
18-seater Volkswagen mini-bus kombi (R750 000):
In terms of section 1, a minibus constructed to carry 16 or more persons is excluded from the definition of "motor car".  |  1
Input tax is therefore not denied under section 17(2)(c).  |  0,5
However, in terms of section 17(1), input tax may only be claimed to the extent goods are acquired for making taxable supplies.  |  1
In terms of section 12(g), the transport by road of fare-paying passengers is an exempt supply.  |  1
The minibus is used exclusively in the passenger transportation offering (exempt), so no input tax can be claimed (input tax = R0).  |  0,5

Required 1.2: Deductibility of the customer settlement (Note 2)
Marking Point / Principle  |  Marks
s11(a) read with s23(g): expenditure and losses, actually incurred, in the year of assessment, in the production of income, not of a capital nature, for purposes of trade.  |  1
A. Cash settlement of R3 000
Actually incurred: paid into Riley's account on 24 March 2025, an unconditional liability in the year (Edgars Stores).  |  1
Production of income: expenditure closely connected to the income-earning operations (Port Elizabeth Electric Tramway).  |  1
Joffe & Co: compensation for negligence is deductible if the risk is an inevitable concomitant of the trade.  |  1
Missing an allergen when preparing food for paying participants is an inherent risk of running cooking classes.  |  1
Paid to settle the claim, avoid litigation and protect the business's goodwill and reputation (Pick 'n Pay Wholesalers).  |  1
Not capital: an ongoing operating cost that creates no enduring asset (New State Areas). Revenue in nature.  |  1
Laid out for purposes of trade (s23(g)).  |  0,5
Conclusion: R3 000 deductible under s11(a).  |  0,5
B. Free class for Riley and ten friends
Expenditure is only actually incurred when there is an unconditional liability (Edgars Stores; Golden Dumps).  |  1
Riley had not booked by 31 October 2025.  |  0,5
No costs (R100 per person) have been incurred; the obligation is contingent on a booking.  |  1
s23(e): no deduction for amounts carried to a provision for future/contingent expenditure.  |  1
Conclusion: no deduction in 2025; the class costs are deductible when actually incurred.  |  0,5
